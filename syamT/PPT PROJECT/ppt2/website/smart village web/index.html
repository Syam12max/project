Education Initiatives / విద్య

1. Digital Classrooms: Equipped with interactive screens and solar power backup in the Mandal Primary School.

2. Scholarship Guidance: Free weekly counseling for higher education and government post-metric scholarships.

3. Adult Literacy: Evening computer awareness classes for farmers and self-help group (SHG) women.

Swachh Village Initiatives

1. Waste Segregation: Dual green/blue bins provided to every household for wet and dry waste separation.

2. Micro-Composting: Organic waste converted into organic fertilizer for agricultural distribution.

3. Drain Cleaning: Bi-weekly desilting of village drains to prevent monsoon stagnation.

Roads & Water Infrastructure

1. Overhead RO Plant: Clean filtered drinking water supplied to 860 household tap connections.

2. Concrete Roads: 4.5 km of weather-resilient cement concrete roads laid across Wards 1 to 5.

3. Solar Street Lights: Automatic dusk-to-dawn LED solar lamps installed along main village thoroughfares.
