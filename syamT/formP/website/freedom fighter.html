Our Great Freedom Fighters

India's freedom was achieved through the courage, sacrifice and determination of many brave people. They worked in different ways to help India become an independent nation.

Famous Freedom Fighters

Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was one of the most important leaders of India's freedom movement. He believed in non-violence and peaceful protest.

Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was a young revolutionary who became an important symbol of courage and sacrifice in India's freedom struggle.

Subhas Chandra Bose

Subhas Chandra Bose was a strong leader who led the Indian National Army and inspired many Indians to fight for freedom.

Rani Lakshmibai

Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi was a brave leader of the 1857 uprising and became a symbol of courage and determination.
